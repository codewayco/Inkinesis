`example-avatars/` catalogs the project's previously generated development characters and contains their small previews. Prepared INP and Live2D files are distributed separately as release attachments and downloaded into that folder on request. No additional asset license is asserted here. Use and redistribution remain subject to the applicable generation-service terms and rights in the supplied inputs. The example catalog and SHA-256 inventory identify the delivered files; private prompts, recordings, intermediate PSDs, experiment logs, and papers are not included.

The README media in `docs/media/` are renderings of these same example characters and follow the same asset terms.

The synthetic fixtures in `tools/test/` are authored procedural test geometry under the project code license. They are not character-generation examples or quality evidence.

Weights and SDK files are obtained separately and must retain their own notices. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).
